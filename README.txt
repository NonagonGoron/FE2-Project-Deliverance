			   ////////ABOUT THIS PATCH\\\\\\\\

This is not a complete overhaul of the Original Artemis Translation,
rather an addendum to make it more polished and in line with the 
official Shadows of Valentia release. A conservative amount of lines
have been rewritten or rephrased, and some have had just a word or 
two changed.

Current Version: 3.5


			   ////////PATCHING INSTRUCTIONS\\\\\\\\

To be applied to any Original Japanese FE2 ROM; Tested on both CRC32-0A187CB4 and E93A6ADB
